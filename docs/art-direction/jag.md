# JAG

Added to Nuke Crew immediately after Big ZX and before WyldWolf. Roster total: 36.

Reference: user-supplied Jag’s Pink Rocket Adventure.png. Built-in image generation/editing was used to isolate the adult character and her complete pink rocket, preserve her brown hair, crown, wink, peace sign, heart-themed costume, white boots, and lettering, and remove scenery, poster text, floating hearts, mascot, and baked exhaust. A second edit requested clean transparency without surrounding glow or haze. The selected image has alpha-zero exterior pixels. Original user reference remains untouched.

Selected source: assets/polished/jag-source.png. Shipping sprite: assets/optimized/jag.webp, alpha-preserving WebP at max 720 pixels. Only crop/resize/format conversion followed the image edits.

Runtime engine: pink layered exhaust at normalized (.065, .75), with boost flare and ember trail. Sprite size 100 by 84 maximum; gameplay collision rules unchanged.

Verified roster order, selection, transparent rendering and boost in the local browser. All 17 tests and the static build pass. No publication.
