# Seasonal maps

Ten new backgrounds generated with the built-in image generation tool, converted to WebP at 1536 by 1024 maximum. Original PNGs retained. The 14-map route interleaves originals, Halloween, autumn, Thanksgiving and spooky winter scenes, changing every eight gates. Local revision only.
